Privacy Policy

Your privacy and information security matter to us. Learn how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website or contact our team.

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the general practices of Trueshield Title Service LLC regarding information submitted through this website. Please review it carefully before using our website or submitting personal information.

1. Introduction

Trueshield Title Service LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website or contact us about our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us or submitting an enquiry, including:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your company name, if provided.
  • The service you are enquiring about.
  • Information included in your message.

Basic technical information may also be processed by website hosting providers or other services used to operate the website.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and service requests.
  • Communicate with potential and existing clients.
  • Understand the services visitors are interested in.
  • Maintain and improve our website and services.
  • Address security concerns and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with authorized service providers when necessary to operate the website or respond to enquiries, or when disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law.

We encourage you not to submit confidential, financial, identity, or other sensitive information through a general website enquiry form.

5. Data Security

We aim to take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies if these features are enabled by the website, its hosting provider, or integrated services. You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings.

7. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may use third-party services, such as website hosting or contact-form processing, and may contain links to external websites. These third parties may process information according to their own policies and practices.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party services before using them.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations. Actual retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and applicable requirements.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to exercise other applicable privacy rights.

To submit a privacy-related enquiry, please contact us using the email address provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business-related enquiries and is not specifically directed at children. Please do not submit children's personal information through this website unless legally authorized to do so.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
